About the artist

Music for the beautiful damage.

Glass & Thorns lives in the space between tenderness and teeth — songs shaped by survival, heartbreak, rage, longing, and the strange hope that keeps growing through the cracks.

For listeners drawn to emotional alternative, post-emo acoustic, dark pop, screamo textures, and heavy cathartic release.
